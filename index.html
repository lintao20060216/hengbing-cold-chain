Simple activation

Water. Freeze.
Pack. Ship.

The absorbent material expands within seconds. Once activated, the process is irreversible and the pack is intended for a single cold-chain cycle.

  1. 01

    Immerse

    Place the dry pack fully under clean water.

  2. 02

    Activate

    Allow a few seconds for the internal material to absorb water and expand.

  3. 03

    Freeze

    Remove surface water and freeze until the pack is completely solid. Time varies by freezer and format.

  4. 04

    Pack

    Place the frozen pack inside suitable insulated packaging for shipment.

Single-use activation: water absorption cannot be reversed. Do not cut, puncture or ingest the contents.

Buyer questions

Clear answers before you order.

Product performance and delivery details can vary by format, packing configuration and destination. We confirm order-specific requirements in writing.

What is a water-activated ice pack?

It is supplied in a compact dry format. When immersed in water, the internal absorbent material expands within seconds. The activated pack is then frozen until solid and used inside suitable insulated packaging.

Can the pack be activated more than once?

No. Water absorption is irreversible. The product is designed for one activation and one cold-chain cycle.

How long should the pack be frozen?

Freeze it until completely solid. The required time depends on the pack size, freezer temperature, loading conditions and equipment performance.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The MOQ for a standard non-customized model is 10 pieces. Custom printing starts from 50,000 pieces.

Can you print our logo?

Yes. We support private-label printing. Plate fees start at CNY 300 per color, plus a CNY 300 machine setup fee. Both are waived for orders of 100,000 pieces or more.

Are samples free and how long is production?

Sample charges, freight and lead time are confirmed for each inquiry according to the selected format, quantity, customization and destination.

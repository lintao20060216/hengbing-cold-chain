Simple activation

Water. Freeze.
Pack. Ship.

Hydrate the dry pack with clean water before freezing. Hydration and freezer times depend on the selected format and operating conditions. Water absorption is irreversible.

  1. 01

    Immerse

    Place the dry pack fully under clean water.

  2. 02

    Activate

    Allow the material to absorb water according to the confirmed instructions and hydration target for your selected format.

  3. 03

    Freeze

    Remove surface water and freeze until the pack is completely solid. Time varies by freezer and format.

  4. 04

    Pack

    Place the frozen pack inside suitable insulated packaging for shipment.

Single-use activation: water absorption cannot be reversed. Do not cut, puncture or ingest the contents.

Buyer questions

Clear answers before you order.

Product performance and delivery details can vary by format, packing configuration and destination. We confirm order-specific requirements in writing.

What is a water-activated ice pack?

It is supplied dry. Hydrate it with clean water according to the confirmed instructions for the selected format, then freeze fully before placing it inside suitable insulated packaging. Hydration time depends on the format and operating conditions.

Can the pack be activated more than once?

No. Water absorption is irreversible. The product is designed for one activation and one cold-chain cycle.

How long should the pack be frozen?

Freeze it until completely solid. The required time depends on the pack size, freezer temperature, loading conditions and equipment performance.

Is 50,000 pieces the minimum order quantity?

No. About 50,000 pieces per model, adjusted to full cartons, is the reference volume for our indicative USD prices. Smaller trial orders and higher-volume programs are quoted individually. Confirm minimum quantities for your chosen specification and customization.

Can you print our logo?

Yes. Custom sizes, logo printing and packaging presentation are available for discussion. Artwork, minimum quantity, sample arrangements and any printing or setup charges are confirmed separately before production.

What do the USD prices include?

Standard dry self-absorbing products and standard carton packing for collection at our Longgang factory. Freight, insurance, loading, export clearance or agency services, destination duties and taxes, and special printing or packing are excluded. Prices are indicative; final terms are confirmed in writing.
